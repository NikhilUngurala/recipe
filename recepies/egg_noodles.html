egg noodles

egg noodles are a type of pasta made from wheat flour and eggs. They are commonly used in various dishes, including soups, stir-fries, and casseroles. Egg noodles have a rich flavor and a tender texture, making them a popular choice in many cuisines around the world.

  • Eggs
  • flour
  • water
  • salt
  • oil
  • sauce

procedure

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the eggs, flour, water, salt, and oil.
  2. Knead the mixture until a smooth dough forms.
  3. Roll out the dough and cut it into your desired noodle shape.
  4. Cook the noodles in boiling water until they float to the surface.
  5. Drain and serve with your favorite sauce or toppings.
