recipe

egg noodles

ingredients

  • rice
  • eggs
  • vegetables
  • soy sauce
  • oil
  • salt

procedure

  1. Cook the rice according to package instructions and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat oil over medium heat.
  3. Add vegetables and stir-fry until tender.
  4. Push the vegetables to the side and scramble the eggs in the center.
  5. Add the cooked rice and soy sauce, mixing everything together.
  6. Season with salt to taste and serve hot.
